How to find a board identifier

Open the employer's career page. For Greenhouse, the board token is commonly in boards.greenhouse.io/BOARD. For Lever, look for jobs.lever.co/BOARD. For Ashby, use jobs.ashbyhq.com/BOARD. SmartRecruiters uses the company identifier from its career page/API.
